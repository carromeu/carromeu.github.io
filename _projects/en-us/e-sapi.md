**e-SAPI / Portal da Carne** (2006 to 2009) was a technology infrastructure for managing traceability and animal health surveillance data in the beef chain, with location-based services and electronic animal transit permits. It was my first software registered at INPI (09399-4, 2007) and the beginning of my work in computing applied to livestock.

**My role:** technical coordination and development, at LEDES/UFMS.
