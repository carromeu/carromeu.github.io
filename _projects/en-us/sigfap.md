The **SIGFAP** family (Information and Management System for Research Support Foundations) grew out of SIGFundect and was instantiated, through a software product line, for research foundations in several Brazilian states (FUNDECT, FAPESPA, FAPEAM, FAPEPI, FAPES). **SIGProj**, a university project management system, was adopted by the Ministry of Education (2009 to 2011).

**My role:** project manager at LEDES/UFMS and author of the product line approach behind the family, the subject of my master's dissertation (2007).
