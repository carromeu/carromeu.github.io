**AndroLógico** is a Progressive Web Application (browser, Android and iOS) that guides veterinarians through the breeding soundness examination of bulls and generates the report for the farmer, working without connectivity in the field.

**My role:** solution architect and technical lead.

**INPI registration:** BR 51 2025 001062-1 (2025).

**Publication:** abstract at the 38th Annual Meeting of the Brazilian Embryo Technology Society (SBTE 2025).
