Two remote sensing projects in which I work as solution architect:

- **SMIDS** (Drone and Satellite Image Monitoring System, 2024 to 2027): agricultural monitoring in the Amazon combining UAV imagery, nanosatellites and artificial intelligence.
- **Pheno Sight**: phenotyping of tropical forages (Panicum maximum and Brachiaria) from aerial imagery and neural networks, supporting Embrapa's breeding programs.

**Scientific basis:** papers in *Sensors* (2020 and 2021) and *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture* (2023) on biomass and dry matter yield estimation from UAV RGB imagery and data fusion.
