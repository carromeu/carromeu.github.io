The **+Precoce Platform** simulates the performance of beef cattle production systems focused on early-slaughter steers, supporting farmers' planning with Embrapa's research results. It was developed in the *+Precoce* project (2014 to 2019) and migrated to a Progressive Web Application.

**My role:** solution architect and technical lead.

**Publications:** CBSoft 2018 Industry Track and SBIAgro 2021.
