**Cria Certo** is a mobile app that helps farmers compare the results of reproductive techniques (natural mating, artificial insemination, fixed-time AI) in their herd, developed in the project of the same name (2015 to 2022).

**My role:** solution architect and technical lead.

**Publication:** abstract in the *Journal of Animal Science* (2020).
