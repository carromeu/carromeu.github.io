**Embrapa I/O** is the platform that standardizes the software engineering practiced across Embrapa's research units: from the GitLab repository to container deployment, through CI/CD pipelines, application boilerplates, release management and production monitoring. Started in 2021, it is now the foundation on which the company's agricultural digital assets are built and maintained.

**My role:** design and technical coordination since the beginning (2021 to date), including the period in which I was Embrapa's Digital Asset Development Supervisor (2022 to 2025).

**Recognition:** Brazil Digital Ozires Silva Award 2022/2023, "National Reference" classification in the "Digital Government" category.

**Components and derivatives:** Embrapa I/O Releaser (command-line client for deploying builds, 2023), per-stack boilerplates, governance guidelines for mobile apps.

**Publication:** extended abstract in the Extended Proceedings of CBSoft 2023.
