The **Titan Framework** was developed at the UFMS Software Engineering Laboratory to quickly instantiate content management systems and web applications from declarative descriptions, within a software product line approach. It was the basis for Titan Architect, UFMS institutional portals and several research funding management systems.

**My role:** creator and main maintainer, from LEDES (2005) to 2022.

**INPI registrations:** versions 1.0 and 2.0 (2014) and 3.0 (2015).

Website: [titanframework.com](https://titanframework.com).
