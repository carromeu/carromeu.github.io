**Pandora** integrated the information systems of Embrapa Beef Cattle to automate research and management processes: core (Pandora-Core), intern and scholarship management (Pandora-Alumnus), quality module, self-service kiosk and other modules.

**My role:** design, architecture and development, since joining Embrapa in 2011.

**INPI registrations:** Pandora-Core (14226-3) and Pandora-Alumnus (14227-5), both from 2012.

**Recognition:** the internship management process automated by Pandora won 1st place in Embrapa's National Team Award (2010).
